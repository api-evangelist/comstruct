# Comstruct

> Comstruct is a Munich-based construction technology company that provides AI-powered agents to automate the procurement of construction materials — from bill of materials (Leistungsverzeichnis) through supplier inquiry, offer comparison, contract negotiation, digital delivery notes, AI invoice processing, and sustainability reporting. Customers include HOCHTIEF, Implenia, Marti, and Leonhard Weiss.

This is an API Evangelist network profile for Comstruct. As of this writing Comstruct publishes no public developer portal, API reference, or OpenAPI definition; this llms.txt is generated from the company profile and the artifacts captured in this repository.
